## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for an interactive educational gamified experience targeting Paket B (SMP equivalent) students. It merges playful digital adventure with an encouraging, culturally grounded academic atmosphere. The visual aesthetic draws inspiration from modern gamified ed-tech interfaces, utilizing cheerful micro-interactions, rich tangible surfaces, rounded silhouettes, and friendly feedback states that alleviate learning anxiety while promoting active curiosity.

Key style pillars:
- **Playful & Tactile:** Interactive elements feature bold, friendly geometry with squishy, touch-first elevation (subtle bottom-beveled button edges and micro-elevations) that feel satisfying to tap on touchscreens.
- **Vibrant & Energizing:** Saturated sky blues paired with golden sun yellows and vibrant citrus accents create an optimistic learning canvas that stimulates engagement without overwhelming visual legibility.
- **Pedagogical Warmth:** Balances whimsical game metaphors (stars, score banners, coin meters) with structured, distraction-free reading containers appropriate for junior high literary exploration.

## Colors

The palette establishes an open, sky-blue canvas energizing the educational narrative, anchored by high-contrast functional hues for clarity and gamified reward moments:

- **Primary Colors:**
  - Sky Blue Active (`#1E88E5`): Main call-to-action buttons, key interactive paths, progress indicators, and active headers.
  - Deep Ocean Blue (`#0D47A1`): Grounding elements, dark contrast text on light containers, prominent navigational borders, and bottom bevel highlights for 3D buttons.
- **Secondary Colors:**
  - Warm Golden Yellow (`#FDD835`): Reward badges, achievement stars, coin counters, and celebratory alert states.
  - Amber Gold Shade (`#F57F17`): Deep accents, shadow fills, borders, and tactile bottom edges for secondary buttons and gold medals.
- **Tertiary & Accent:**
  - Energetic Orange (`#FB8C00`): Quest banners, streak badges, interactive tooltips, and time-sensitive mini-game triggers.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Pure Canvas White (`#FFFFFF`): Card backgrounds, modal sheets, and high-readability answer tiles.
  - Cloud Slate (`#F4F8FC`): Background canvas and off-white base layer.
  - Dark Charcoal Ink (`#263238`): High-legibility text neutral providing optimal contrast against pure white cards.

## Typography

The design system incorporates **Times New Roman** across headlines, interactive labels, and body text. In an Indonesian language learning interface for Paket B, this provides an authoritative, bookish, and formal literary feeling that mimics real Indonesian language textbooks (*Bahasa dan Sastra Indonesia*), while gamified UI shells supply contemporary levity and playfulness.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with bold weights (`700`), delivering crisp legibility for level titles, quest markers, and comprehension prompts.
- **Reading Passages (Body):** Regular weights (`400`) with generous line-heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) ensure long text passages, Indonesian fables, and dialogues remain effortlessly readable on hand-held tablet devices and desktop screens.
- **Interactive Labels:** Semi-bold to bold weights for buttons and badges maintain contrast against colorful and vibrant backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a fluid, responsive 12-column grid for wide viewports and scales to a 4-column system for handheld devices, optimizing for one-handed thumb interaction and two-handed tablet touch zones:

- **Desktop & Landscape Tablets (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width `1200px`, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Two-column split interfaces allow reading passages on the left and interactive gamified quiz modules on the right.
- **Tablets & Small Screens (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`. Stacked layout prioritizing big answer blocks and clear sticky footer navigation.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built on an 8pt increment model. Component gaps use `space-md` (`1rem`) to keep hit targets distinct and prevent accidental taps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tactile gamified layers and tinted ambient shadows**, combining soft light projection with solid 3D-styled bottom edges:

- **Gamified Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles):** `0px 4px 14px rgba(30, 136, 229, 0.08)` paired with an inner crisp white border (`1px solid #E3F2FD`) providing a clear, clean boundary.
- **Gamified Level 2 (Floating Modals, Reward Sheets & Badges):** `0px 8px 24px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.14)`.
- **Tactile Button Press Depths:** Buttons utilize solid simulated extrusion via inset/offset shadows:
  - Sky Blue Buttons: `0 4px 0 #0D47A1` (compressed to `0 1px 0 #0D47A1` on `:active` with `translateY(3px)`).
  - Golden Reward Buttons: `0 4px 0 #F57F17` (compressed to `0 1px 0 #F57F17` on `:active` with `translateY(3px)`).
- **Glass Floating Meters:** Translucent HUD bars (XP, Coins, Level progress) utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a `12px` backdrop blur to float unobtrusively over map stages.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a friendly, rounded aesthetic (`level 2`) to ensure soft silhouettes that feel inviting to junior students:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Rounded corners (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`) remove visual stiffness from academic exercises.
- **Interactive Tiles & Answer Blocks:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` radius, creating distinct touch surfaces with comfortable click perimeters.
- **Pill Badges & Meters:** Score pills, coin counters, and streak badges utilize full pill radii (`9999px`) to emphasize their collectible, coin-like nature.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Adventure CTA):** Saturated Sky Blue (`#1E88E5`) background, White bold text, `0.75rem` border radius, minimum height of `52px` for touch accessibility. Features a solid `4px` bottom rim (`#0D47A1`) that snaps down when pressed.
- **Reward / Secondary Action:** Golden Yellow (`#FDD835`) background with deep amber label (`#5D3B00`) and `#F57F17` bottom border.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** White background with a `2px` Sky Blue border and Sky Blue text.

### 2. Gamified Score Badges & Coin Meters
- **Header HUD Bar:** Floating pill container with white background and soft blue shadow.
- **Coin & Star Badge:** Bright Golden Yellow (`#FDD835`) or Energetic Orange (`#FB8C00`) icon pill containing an animated 3D star/coin icon, paired with high-contrast bold score typography (`Times New Roman`, `700`).

### 3. Quest & Educational Cards
- **Reading Passage Card:** White canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1.5rem` internal padding, subtle sky blue boundary (`#E3F2FD`), and soft elevated shadow.
- **Quiz Question Card:** Features a top banner highlighted in Deep Ocean Blue (`#0D47A1`) or Sky Blue (`#1E88E5`), followed by a roomy content slot for text passages and comprehension questions.

### 4. Selection Tiles (Multiple Choice & Drag-and-Drop)
- **Resting:** `#FFFFFF` background, `2px` solid `#E0E0E0` border, `1rem` rounded corners, generous `1rem` padding.
- **Hover/Focus:** `#E3F2FD` background with a `#1E88E5` border.
- **Selected:** Vibrant Sky Blue tint (`#BBDEFB`) with `#1E88E5` border and a checkmark badge.
- **Correct State:** Mint green tint (`#E8F5E9`) with `#2E7D32` border and star explosion micro-animation.
- **Incorrect State:** Coral tint (`#FFEBEE`) with `#C62828` border accompanied by an encouraging hint modal.

### 5. Progress Bars & Level Paths
- **Level Progress:** Thick rounded track (`16px` height) with Cloud Slate base (`#E1F5FE`) and a glossy animated Sky Blue to Golden Yellow filling indicator.
- **Quest Waypoints:** Circular interactive nodes (`48px x 48px`) connected by dashed path lines representing the educational expedition throughout the Indonesian archipelago.
